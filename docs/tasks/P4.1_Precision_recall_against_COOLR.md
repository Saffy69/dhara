# P4.1 — Precision/recall against COOLR

P4.1 — Precision/recall against COOLR
Write a validation script that takes the list of Change Passports produced for the
Nepal AOI's historical (Sentinel-1) time window, and the COOLR ground-truth GeoJSON
from P1.4, and computes:
1. Precision: what fraction of our detected clusters spatially and temporally overlap
   (within a documented distance and time tolerance — state and justify both) a real
   COOLR event.
2. Recall: what fraction of real COOLR events in our AOI/window were matched by at
   least one of our detected clusters.
3. For every matched pair, the detection lead time in days (negative if our detection
   came after the reported event date, positive if before).
Output a clear results table and a written, honest paragraph explaining any false
positives and false negatives with a plausible scientific reason (e.g., "COOLR likely
under-reports in this remote district due to sparse citizen-science coverage" or "this
false positive cluster overlaps a known agricultural terrace, consistent with a
seasonal soil-moisture signal rather than slope failure").

Acceptance criteria: precision and recall are computed from real data, not asserted;
the false positive/negative explanation is specific to the actual cases found, not a
generic disclaimer; if recall or precision is very low, the script says so plainly
rather than the surrounding narrative overselling the number.
