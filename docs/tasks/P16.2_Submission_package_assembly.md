P16.2 — Submission package assembly
Assemble the final submission package: confirm the README, methodology doc, live demo
link, GitHub repo link, and video are all present, current, and cross-linked correctly;
do a final pass confirming nothing in any submitted material claims a feature, number,
or partnership that isn't real and verifiable by a judge who checks. Remove or clearly
caveat anything that can't survive that check.

Acceptance criteria: every single claim in every submitted document and in the pitch
script can be traced, by the team, to a specific real file, number, or deployed feature
— if any claim can't be traced this way, it is cut or rewritten before submission.
