P15.2 — Mock Q&A preparation
Generate a list of the 10 hardest questions a NASA scientist judge could plausibly ask
about this project (e.g., "how do you know your confidence weights aren't overfit to a
handful of COOLR events", "what happens when NISAR and Sentinel-1 disagree over the
same pixel", "how would a real disaster office actually receive an alert from this
today"), and draft a short, honest, specific answer to each based on what was actually
built — including answers that honestly admit a limitation where that's the true
answer, rather than deflecting.

Acceptance criteria: the team does a live rehearsal where someone else asks these
questions cold and the presenter can answer each in under 30 seconds without reading
from a script.
