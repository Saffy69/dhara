# P14.1 — Draft the ICIMOD / SERVIR-HKH outreach message

P14.1 — Draft the ICIMOD / SERVIR-HKH outreach message
Draft a short, respectful, specific outreach email (under 200 words) to ICIMOD's
SERVIR-HKH program, introducing our NASA Space Apps Challenge project (DHARA), briefly
describing what it does (2-3 sentences), and asking one specific, answerable question —
for example, whether a confidence-decomposed evidence object like our Change Passport
matches what a district disaster officer could realistically act on, based on their
team's experience building landslide monitoring/forecasting systems for Nepal. Include
a link to our GitHub repo and, if ready, the live demo. Do not ask them to do work for
us, review our code, or commit time — frame this purely as a request for a brief
reaction or pointer to relevant prior work, and make clear we don't expect or need a
reply to proceed.

Acceptance criteria: the email is honest about what we've built (do not overstate
progress to make the ask seem more impressive), specific enough to answer in under 5
minutes of someone else's time, and sent no later than week 2 of the roadmap.
