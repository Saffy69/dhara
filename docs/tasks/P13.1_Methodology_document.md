P13.1 — Methodology document
Write a methodology document (docs/METHODOLOGY.md) covering, in this order: the
scientific problem and why NISAR is suited to it; the exact data products and sources
used, with real dates/CRIDs pulled from our actual manifest files (Phase 1), not
generic descriptions; the confidence model formula and component definitions, exactly
as implemented; the validation methodology and results from Phase 4, including the
honest false-positive/negative discussion; and a clearly labeled "Limitations" section
covering at minimum: NISAR's limited historical depth, the S-band exclusion decision
and why, the risk of threshold overfitting to a small validation set, and the
line-of-sight-only nature of the displacement measurements.

Acceptance criteria: every number in this document is traceable to a real pipeline
output file, not written from memory or estimation; a NISAR scientist reading this
document should find no scientific claim they'd need to correct.
