P14.2 — Draft a fallback local outreach message
Draft a similar short outreach message (under 200 words) to Nepal's National Disaster
Risk Reduction and Management Authority or a relevant municipal disaster office as a
fallback/parallel option if ICIMOD/SERVIR-HKH does not respond in time, adjusting the
technical framing to be even more plain-language and result-focused (lead with "would a
tool like this be useful to your office" rather than technical confidence-model
language).

Acceptance criteria: a native or fluent Nepali speaker on the team reviews this message
for tone and cultural appropriateness before it is sent.
