# P5.2 — Flood/water extent change detection

P5.2 — Flood/water extent change detection
Using the GCOV-derived water masks from P2.2 across multiple dates, write a function
that computes water extent area per date, flags significant extent increases
(documented threshold, e.g. >X% area growth between consecutive observations) as
candidate flood/lake-expansion events, and produces Change Passport objects with
hazard_type "flood_extent_change". Tie this explicitly, in the passport's
recommended_next_step field, to the glacial-lake-outburst-flood narrative for the
Melamchi story where geographically relevant.

Acceptance criteria: real water-extent time series exist for at least one lake/river
segment in the AOI, and at least one schema-valid flood_extent_change Change Passport
is produced from real data.
