# P15.3 — Demo video production

P15.3 — Demo video production
Plan and script a 2-3 minute demo video: screen-recorded walkthrough of Story Mode
finding a real event, opening its Change Passport, and a brief Research Mode flash
showing real provenance and validation numbers, voiced over with the same core
narrative arc as the live pitch script (P15.1) but tightened for video pacing. Record
against the live deployed URL, not localhost, so the video matches exactly what a judge
clicking the link themselves will see.

Acceptance criteria: the video is recorded from the real live deployment, is under the
challenge's stated time/format limits, and does not show any placeholder or mock data
anywhere in the frame.
